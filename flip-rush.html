About Flip Rush

Flip Rush: Best physics-based stunt sports game where time is crucial! Take control of a bold ragdoll athlete and test your skills in exciting surroundings. Prepare to execute some amazing flips, defy gravity, and land perfectly for absolute victory from interior gyms to bustling cities!

Flip Rush - A Parkour & Stunt Sports Game Based On Physics

The whole concept of Flip Rush is to jump, spin, and land perfectly. Each level starts on a platform, and you need to make a jump to a certain goal area. While in the air, you can do fantastic midair spins to score huge points. But the actual problem is landing! You have to land on your feet and within the marked area. A bad landing is an instant mission failure. The closer you land to the center of the zone, the bigger the point multiplier! To complete the level, you need to at least reach the silver goal. If you beat all the levels in a stage, you unlock whole new jumping spots for increased challenge.

Flip Rush Controls

  • Click & Hold (or Spacebar): Squat and ready your character for a jump.
  • Release (On Platform): Launch your ragdoll athlete into the sky.
  • Hold Again (Mid-Air): Tuck your body to rotate rapidly and do amazing flips.
  • Release (Before Touchdown): Straighten yourself to set yourself up for a safe, upright landing on your feet.

Epic Features, Unlocks & Pro Tips

  • 11 Unique Stages: Experience a variety of fun and unique places, including The Gym, Mountain, City, and Funhouse with new difficulties to overcome.
  • Scout Your Target: Before you jump, always take a brief glance at your landing zone to aim correctly.
  • Focus on the Touchdown: Don’t gamble for an extra flip without the height. Release your tuck early to land properly on both feet!
  • Get Instant Replayability: Made a mistake? Restart the level and try a different approach fast!
